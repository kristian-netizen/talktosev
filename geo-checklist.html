Tools and Resources

Schema Generators
schema.org/docs/schemas, merkle.com/structured-data-tester. Use these to generate Article, FAQPage, and Author schemas. Paste the output into your site head.
Schema Validation
search.google.com/test/rich-results. Confirms whether AI engines and Google can read your structured data correctly.
AI Citation Trackers
profound.so, otterly.ai, superlines.io, airops.com. Monitor where your brand gets cited across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Copilot.
Performance Testing
pagespeed.web.dev, gtmetrix.com. Verify Core Web Vitals and page speed before assuming the work is done.
Robots.txt Reference
Allow GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, OAI-SearchBot, anthropic-ai, ccbot, and FacebookBot. Block only what you genuinely need to keep private.
Buyer Question Mining
reddit.com/r/[your industry], answerthepublic.com, alsoasked.com. Find the actual questions your buyers type before they buy.
